# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Rugby:** 48 nations in 6 leagues of 8. Tries, conversions, penalties, drop goals and sin bins; bonus points; kicking contests in knockouts; matches from 1 minute
- **Cricket (T20):** 30 nations in 5 leagues of 6, including the West Indies. Follow it ball by ball on a real scorecard, with this over; wickets and the chase ("need 34 from 18 balls"); net run rate; super overs; run scorers and wicket takers; matches from 2 minutes
- Players in every sport now get the most common names in their own country, with no mixing between countries and real stars' names blocked. Squads in a career already under way keep their names until those players leave
- A nation's players now and then take a surname from a neighbour: England from Wales, Scotland and Ireland, and so on
- Each sport has its own career, cups, summer questions and records
- Play several sports at once: the box lists them, one open in full and the others minimised to one line. Start, pause or end each on its own, and each keeps its own match length
- **Honours:** past careers, greatest teams, all-time records for each sport, and honours for success across sports
- Only want one sport? Tick the ones you want in the settings, and turn the Honours row off too if you like. A hidden career waits where it was
- End a career to keep it in Past careers, or reset it to delete it completely, both from one button that explains the difference. **Reset honours** clears the Honours page. Both resets ask again in red
- **Cricket:** the further down the order, the sooner a batter gets out and the slower they score, and matches show a real scorecard: how each batter got out, runs and balls, and the bowling figures
- Each sport has its own coloured border, and the box is tighter and clearer throughout
- Football and rugby match reports now put each team's events on its own side, like the scoreboard
- Minimised sports show the league week and a thin line for how far through the season they are
- The end-of-season message is now one short line that goes by itself
- Matches against your rival are called out in the status bar, on the scoreboard and at full time, and the season report shows how you did against them

## 0.1.0

First release.

- International football career: about 210 nations to play as, 100 of them in a world of 5 leagues with promotion, play-offs and relegation
- Nations Cup every season and a World Championship every fourth summer, with their own Cups tab
- Live matches, match report and live tables in the Explorer sidebar, with your score in the status bar (GOAL! when one goes in, and a hover with your position and expected finish)
- Collapse the box and its title still shows your position and score; a badge marks the summer choice
- With several VS Code windows open, the game runs in the one you're working in
- Setup: country (type to search), starting league and match length; one standard game, no difficulty settings
- One decision a season, asked a different way each summer (call up a player, appoint a coach, change formation, set the training camp), read from where you ranked for goals scored and conceded and from who's retiring or being dropped
- No ceiling at 99: a great manager can keep climbing and chase an unbeaten season
- A lopsided team declines, and recovers, over a few seasons rather than overnight
- Season reports, trophy cabinet, club history and records
